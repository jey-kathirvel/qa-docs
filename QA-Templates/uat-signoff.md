## Acceptance Criteria
The system is eligible for sign-off when:
- Business-critical workflows have been tested.
- Required test cases have passed or accepted exceptions are documented.
- No unresolved critical defects remain.
- Stakeholders agree the system is fit for purpose [web:173][web:180][web:182].
